Gabriel's Reach

  • You lead the Aurum Concord. Two rivals share this cluster. Be the last empire standing.
  • Ships travel in fleets along hyperlanes, one jump per turn. A fleet of only scouts makes two. Unknown stars are charted when a fleet comes within one jump.
  • Tap a star, then Send… on a fleet. Choose how many of each ship to send, then tap the destination. The rest stay behind as their own fleet. Merge joins fleets parked at the same star. Orders run when you end the turn.
  • Stars have up to five planets. Star colour hints at what you will find: yellow and orange stars have the best worlds, blue-white and red stars mostly barren ones and gas giants. Tap a star twice, or Enter system, to see its planets.
  • Colony ships only settle when you choose a planet for them: tap a free planet and press Colonise. Until then they wait in orbit. Colonies grow, and each person yields 1 industry per turn.
  • Spend industry on ships and research. Fighters attack anything hostile. Fighters in a system besiege one undefended enemy planet a turn: the one you pick with Attack, or else the weakest. Planets shoot back.
  • Touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to rotate. Mouse: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag or Shift-drag to rotate. Esc cancels a send or leaves a system.
