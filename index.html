Gabriel's Reach

New here? The tutorial plays the first turns with you, one step at a time, in a gentle galaxy.

  • You lead the Aurum Concord. An empire falls when it has no planets and no colony ships.
  • Each turn, give your orders, then press End turn. Everything happens at once: your orders, the rivals' orders, battles, sieges, growth.
  • Industry (IU) pays for everything. You earn 2 IU a turn plus 1 for every person on your planets. Unspent IU carries over.
  • Fog of war: you see one jump around your planets and fleets. Elsewhere the map shows what you last saw, which may be out of date.
  • Your game saves itself in this browser after every move. The Game tab saves it to a file.

  • Ships travel in fleets along the hyperlanes, one jump a turn, or two for a fleet of only scouts. Each Hyperdrives level adds one. A fleet moves at the pace of its slowest ship.
  • Send… on a fleet: choose how many of each ship, press Choose destination, tap a star. The ships you chose split off as a new fleet; the rest stay.
  • Merge joins fleets parked at the same star. Hold cancels a move. Undo removes your last order.
  • Build ships in the Shipyard of any star where you hold a planet. New ships join a parked fleet there at the end of the turn and can move the turn after.
  • Shipyard capacity: each system launches at most 1 ship a turn, plus 1 for every 4 people on your planets there. A homeworld of 3 launches 1 a turn; at 8 people it launches 3. A newly captured planet launches only 1, so big fleets come from your populous core and have to travel to the front. Rivals follow the same rule.
  • Each star has up to five planets. Tap a star twice, or press Enter system, to see them. ‹ Galaxy or Esc goes back.
  • Star colour hints at what you will find:
YellowRich: terran and ocean worlds
OrangeGood: many habitable planets
WhiteMixed
Blue-whitePoor: barren, toxic, gas giants
RedPoor: cold and small
  • Habitability is the most people a planet can hold. 0 means nobody can live there. Terraforming raises it.
  • To colonise, get a fleet with a colony ship into the system or on its way there, tap a free planet and press Colonise. Colony ships never settle on their own.
  • Colonies grow by 1 person every other turn up to their habitability. Each person earns 1 IU a turn, and every 4 people in a system let its shipyard launch one more ship a turn.
  • On the map, each star shows a dot per planet: an empire's colour if owned, white if free and habitable, dim if nobody can live there.
  • Rivals can hold planets in the same system as you.
  • Battles happen when ships end a turn at a star where another empire has ships or inhabited planets. Fleets passing through a star do not stop to fight.
  • Each fighter fires once a round, for up to 6 rounds. Each inhabited planet fires 1 shot plus 1 for every 3 people, in the first round only. Scouts and colony ships never shoot.
  • A shot hits 40% of the time, plus 10% for each Weapons level. Fighters are shot first; colony ships and scouts only once their fighters are gone.
  • Sieges: after the battle, if a rival has no fighters left in the system, your fighters remove ½ person per fighter (rounded up) from one of its planets each turn. At 0 the planet is yours, with 1 person.
  • Choose which planet with Attack: enter the system, tap the enemy planet, press Attack on a fleet that is there or on its way. Without a choice, fighters hit the weakest planet.
  • Rule of thumb: bring at least as many fighters as the planet has people, and it falls in about two turns. Leave a fighter behind to guard what you take; any fighter of yours in a system protects all your planets there.

  • Weapons before a big war. Terraforming once you hold several colonies. Hyperdrives when your empire is spread out.
  • Touch: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to rotate.
  • Mouse: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag or Shift-drag to rotate.
  • Tap a star to select it; tap it again to enter its system. Tap a planet to see it.
  • Tap a line in the turn report to jump to that star.
  • Esc cancels a send, closes a panel, or leaves a system.
